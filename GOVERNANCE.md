# Governance

Open Research is currently an independent, founder-led initiative maintained by [Rtx09x](https://github.com/Rtx09x). The founder initially coordinates admission, repository standards, disputes and infrastructure. Decisions should be based on relevance, evidence, quality and constructive participation, not institutional prestige.

Project maintainers direct their research, choose methods and organize their folders within the minimum contribution standards. Maintainership does not confer exclusive ownership of ideas or authority to suppress valid criticism. Contributors retain attribution and copyright in their original work, subject to the applicable open licence.

As participation grows, additional maintainers and qualified reviewers may be appointed with transparent scopes of responsibility. Reviewers should disclose material conflicts and distinguish independent checks from authors' own checks. Decisions and corrections should be documented in issues, pull requests or project records where appropriate.

The central maintainers may request changes, reject unsuitable submissions or restrict conduct that violates the code of conduct. Disputed scientific claims should be investigated openly and labelled accurately. Appeals may be raised with repository maintainers.

This document describes the current structure, not a claim of institutional affiliation or formal academic accreditation.
