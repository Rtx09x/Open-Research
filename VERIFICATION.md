# Research verification

Admission into Open Research is not endorsement of scientific correctness. Research progress and verification are separate, and claims within a project may have different evidence levels.

## Verification status

Use one of these exact values in the project README, and optionally display a corresponding Shields.io badge.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Unverified | Preliminary or conjectural; substantive validation not yet established. |
| Internally verified | Authors performed documented substantive checks; independent verification is still pending. |
| Under independent review | An independent reviewer is actively examining the claim. |
| Independently verified | Independent checking or reproduction supports the specified claim; scope and evidence are linked. |
| Peer reviewed | A specified claim appeared in a formally peer-reviewed publication; cite it. |
| Disputed | A material unresolved objection exists; link the objection and response. |

For mixed results, use a conservative overall status and record claim-specific statuses separately. Peer review and reproduction are different kinds of evidence; neither guarantees correctness. A disputed claim can have prior review history.

Example badge: `![Verification: Internally verified](https://img.shields.io/badge/verification-internally%20verified-orange)`

## Evidence and updates
Document the claim, assumptions, method, inputs, software versions where applicable, checks, limitations and known failures. For independent verification, identify the reviewer or public review record, checked version, scope, method and outcome. AI output or repeated runs by the authors alone do not establish independent verification.

Record research progress separately: proposed, active, partial, completed or archived. Completed refers only to the stated objective. Update status when evidence changes; preserve superseded findings and correction history. Do not silently remove substantive objections.

A repository workflow checks organization, not scientific truth.
