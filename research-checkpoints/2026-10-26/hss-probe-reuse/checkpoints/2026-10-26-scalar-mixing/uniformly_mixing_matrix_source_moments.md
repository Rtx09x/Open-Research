# Deterministic matrix-source moments under uniformly mixing transfers

**Status:** complete restricted proof; independent analytic advisor audit PASS
(26 October 2026).
This is NOT arbitrary-basis reconstruction. It controls a deterministic source
matrix, not a bank-correlated anchor or the source operator's norm.

Use the explicit full-completion, independent Haar tree-frame construction and
pure-source reverse-coordinate law from the audited matrix branch reduction.
Let d=s-k>=2k. Along the live ancestor branch assume all prescribed transfer
blocks obey

    sigma_min(T),sigma_min(B)>=delta>0.

The orthogonal full transfers imply ||R||,||A||<=rho=sqrt(1-delta²)<1.
This is a genuine restriction: coordinate/weak-mixing transfers are excluded.
Set

    c=min(delta^4,(1-k/d)delta²),
    Bdelta=[1+k/(2d(1-rho^4))]/c.

For every deterministic source matrix M, let V_j(M) include its own local
ad or da coefficient and its full strict-ancestor/root response. Then

    E||V_j(M)||_F² <=(1+Bdelta)||M||_F²,
    E||V_j(M)||_F^4 <=[1+2Bdelta+242 Bdelta²]||M||_F^4.       (1)

Constants depend on delta, but not k, depth, or matrix size. For example at
d=2k and delta=1/sqrt2, Bdelta=16/3. No uniform delta-free bound follows.

## 1. Summable flux and active common mode

In the matrix branch notation write D=X-Z, C=(X+Z)/2 and
H=||D||_F²+||Y||_F²+||W||_F². The exact expected flux loss gives

    E[H'|state]<= (1-c)H.

Indeed ||T^T D B||²>=delta^4||D||², and both passive loss terms are at least
(1-k/d)delta² times their respective energies. Pointwise H'<=H also holds.

The active updates yield

    C'=R^T C A+(R^T Ybar A_perp+R_perp^T Wbar A)/2.

The two fresh complement rotations are independent and centered. Haar
isotropy and the transfer norm bounds therefore give

    E[||C'||²|state]<=rho^4||C||²+(k/(4d))H.               (2)

Write J=||X||²+||Z||²+||Y||²+||W||²<=2||C||²+H.
The expected emitted coefficient energy is at most J, by the exact local
cost formula. The terminal forward root decoder is a contraction, so its
energy is at most the terminal J. Summing the finite recurrences, including
that terminal state, gives the all-state complete-output estimate

    E||output||²
      <=2||C_0||²/(1-rho^4)+Bdelta H_0.                  (3)

In particular a pure passive source has C_0=0,H_0=||M||², proving the first
bound(1) after adding its orthogonal birth coefficient. Formula(3) also shows
an all-state budget in terms of J_0 with constant
1/(1-rho^4)+2Bdelta.

## 2. A self-contained Haar fourth-moment fact

For a uniform O in O(d), d>=2, and any deterministic real matrix A,

    E<tr(AO)>^4
      =3[(d+1)(tr AA^T)^2-2tr((AA^T)^2)]/[d(d-1)(d+2)]
      <=3(E<tr(AO)>²)^2.                                 (4)

By two-sided invariance take A diagonal with singular values a_i. Row-sign
symmetry leaves only the individual fourth powers and pairwise squared
products. For a uniform sphere row v, rotational invariance gives
E v_1^4=3/[d(d+2)] and E v_1²v_2²=1/[d(d+2)]. Conditional on the first row,
the second row is isotropic in its orthogonal complement, so
E O_11²O_22²=(d+1)/[d(d-1)(d+2)]. Expansion gives(4); its inequality uses
tr((AA^T)^2)>=(tr AA^T)^2/d.

For any Hilbert-valued deterministic linear map L(O), apply(4) to each scalar
output coordinate and Cauchy–Schwarz to coordinate pairs. This yields
E||L(O)||^4<=3(E||L(O)||²)^2. The same bound holds for the sum of two independent
centered such maps, by expansion and Cauchy–Schwarz. All are centrally symmetric.
No Gaussian mgf comparison or asymptotic Haar approximation is being used.

## 3. Flux-only Doob variation and the fourth moment

For the complete strict-ancestor/root coefficient vector Z generated by the
pure deterministic source, reveal the valid reverse-coordinate orientations.
Its Doob differences are deterministic linear maps of the newly rotated
passive matrices. The conditional future mean depends only on X,Z, and its
variation depends only on D,Y,W, as established in the exact matrix reduction.
Thus resetting the common mode C to zero preserves the entire future
predictable-variation law. The reset has the same flux H.

Let a_i be a predictable squared increment, and Avar=sum_i a_i. Conditional
on a pre-step history, (3) applied to the reset gives the INCLUSIVE budget

    E[sum_(j>=i) a_j | past]<=Bdelta H_i<=Bdelta||M||².

Therefore EAvar²<=2 Bdelta²||M||^4, using the same predictable-tail identity
as the scalar proof. By(4), each Hilbert increment has conditional fourth
moment at most3 a_i². Central symmetry removes both odd terms in the fourth-
power martingale expansion. The dimension-free Doob L4 calculation from the
scalar proof then gives

    E||Z||^4 <=121 EAvar² <=242 Bdelta²||M||^4.

The unit source coefficient M has orthogonal support from every strict
ancestor/root output; adding it gives the second inequality(1).

## Boundary of the statement

The input M is deterministic before both banks. Substituting a random
opposite-bank anchor M is not justified. The separately refuted uniform
one-bank-conditional shortcut remains false when its transfer restriction
is absent; no such shortcut is used here. Nor does (1) give a fourth moment
of the operator norm M->V_j(M) without a possible dimension loss. An arbitrary
rank-k constant-risk coefficient theorem and an unknown-basis learner remain
open. This conditional source lemma primarily isolates the weak-mixing
transfer regime as a quantitative difficulty, not as an impossibility.
