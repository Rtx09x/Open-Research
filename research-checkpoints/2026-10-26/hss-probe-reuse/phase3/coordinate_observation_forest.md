# Exact risk weights for independently chosen coordinate row/column bases

**Status:** exact structural reduction; independent advisor analytic audit passed.
This concerns fixed scalar coordinate nested bases, not general dense bases
or a query-efficient basis learner. The row and column coordinate choices
may be unrelated. It is not an HSS common-optimum theorem.

## Coordinates, colors, and support

At every nonroot node, select one of its two current row representatives as
active and the other as detail. Select column representatives independently.
There are three free coefficients: da=(row detail,column active),
dd=(row detail,column detail), ad=(row active,column detail). The root's2x2
core has four free coefficients. This is a coordinate support class C with
3n−2 free entries, for n=2^(L+1).

Assign colors0,1 to the two root row representatives. Descending the hierarchy,
a row detail receives the opposite color from its corresponding active row.
Do this independently for column representatives. Denote the physical row
colors r_i and column colors c_j. Square two-column tree probes have rows

    Psi_i=tau_i e_(r_i)^T,   Omega_j=sigma_j e_(c_j)^T,

with signs generated by independent scalar Haar complements. Independent
signs may also be placed on the two root rows: they only rotate the two
probe columns orthogonally and do not change the decoder's distribution.
With this harmless augmentation the physical vertex signs tau_i and sigma_j
are independent uniform signs. The triangular root-to-detail change from
complement signs to physical signs is invertible.

## The observation-channel forest

Create4n vertices, one for every scalar forward observation F(i,c), c=0,1,
and every transpose observation T(j,r), r=0,1. A supported coefficient(i,j)
is an edge joining F(i,c_j) to T(j,r_i). Mark a chosen endpoint for each edge:
forward for da,dd and all root coefficients; transpose for ad.

Starting at the root, its four edges are isolated components: row and column
root colors are distinct. Adding a finer node introduces two previously
absent row-detail observation vertices and two previously absent column-detail
observation vertices. The da edge joins one new forward vertex to an old
transpose vertex. The ad edge joins one old forward vertex to one new
transpose vertex. The dd edge joins the remaining new vertices and starts
an isolated edge component. Consequently the graph is a forest with3n−2
edges and n+2 components. This holds even though the PHYSICAL support graph
need not be a forest.

Each edge's chosen endpoint is its child in a rooted orientation. Every
component has exactly one unused endpoint equation, called its anchor: the
transpose endpoint of the component's root coefficient or dd coefficient.
Adding da/ad edges attaches a chosen child to an existing parent; dd starts
a new component with its transpose endpoint as anchor.

## LM is rooted tree-flow inversion

Normalize a forward observation by multiplying it by tau_i and a transpose
observation by multiplying it by−sigma_j. Normalize the unknown coefficient
B_ij by tau_i sigma_j. Then the selected observation equations are exactly
the signed divergence equations on this forest, imposed at every nonanchor
vertex. The ordinary LM eliminate/subtract/compress recursion solves these
equations by leaf elimination. Finer coefficients incident to an observation
are subtracted before its chosen edge is recovered, and the root step uses
the prescribed forward equations. This identifies the actual decoder, not
merely some exact-recovery algorithm with the same query count.

For an off-support unit input e_i e_j^T, the only two normalized nonzero
observations are opposite charges of magnitude1 at

    u=F(i,c_j),   v=T(j,r_i).

If u,v share a component, the unique flow travels along their path. If they
belong to different components, each charge travels to its component anchor;
the anchor equation is not imposed. Hence the output coefficient energy is
pointwise, for every sign realization,

    w_ij = distance(u,v),                         same component;
    w_ij = distance(u,anchor(u))+distance(v,anchor(v)), otherwise.    (1)

The signs only conjugate coefficient signs. This immediately handles arbitrary
left/right coordinate choices and arbitrary component anchors.

## Exact expected error identity

The fixed coordinate class and probe law are invariant under independent
physical row and column signs; LM is equivariant. Thus the expected squared
output norm is diagonal in physical matrix units. Equivalently, in the flow
formula the coefficient from input(i,j) to a fixed output(a,b) has sign
factor tau_i sigma_j tau_a sigma_b, so distinct input cross terms average to
zero. For E=A−P_C A the output lies in C and is orthogonal to E. Therefore

    E_sign ||A−R_C(A)||_F^2
       = sum_(i,j off support) (1+w_ij)|A_ij|^2.                 (2)

This is an exact deterministic weighted-entry expression, not an upper birth
ledger. When C is the output of a full-entry coordinate-valued greedy learner,
(2) isolates the remaining common-optimum question: does this weighted-entry
norm admit an O(L) bound against unrestricted rank-one HSS optimum?

## Exact controls

`coordinate_observation_forest.py` independently constructs the graph and a
physical2x2 integer LM decoder acting on ALL matrix units simultaneously.
It checks every16 coordinate row/column hierarchy pairs at n4 and every4096
pairs at n8. All unit-input energies equal(1), exact on-model recovery holds,
and the chosen-endpoint graph is a forest with n+2 components. It also checks
the previously audited greedy counterexample at L3,...,8: every rejected
signal unit has w=3, giving actual total error4L, rather than a quadratic
weighted birth certificate. These finite checks corroborate the structural
proof but are not substitutes for it.

## Block-coordinate extension

The same argument extends to kxk coordinate blocks that choose an entire child
block as active, with four-k queries. Replace signs by independent orthogonal
block orientations and normalize a coefficient as T_i^T B_ij S_j. Divergence
and leaf elimination become matrix-valued; each path preserves Frobenius norm.
Independent central signs kill cross-input-block terms. Thus(2) becomes
sum(1+w_ij)||A_ij||_F^2 for off-support blocks. This does not extend to dense
mixing between the two child blocks.
