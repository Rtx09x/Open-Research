## Why the source theorem still applies

AH's small-class container proportions may be chosen as nonnegative constants summing to one. Only an already-positive small-class type mass is changed, so all allowed container sizes and the entire scenario partition stay unchanged. Large-threshold feasibility conditions (1), (2), (4), (6) involve only u,v,w and the two proportions of that **large threshold class**, not the proportions of class 172. They therefore remain valid; the complete verifier checks them anyway.

For every scenario, construct the exact item weights and tiny density by §§4.3–4.4 of the source. Solve the two strict-infimum knapsack problems. The source weight theorem gives ALG(I)≤weight(I)+C, with C independent of input length. The pricing certificates give weight(I)≤R·OPT(I). Thus ALG(I)≤R·OPT(I)+C for every input, proving the stated asymptotic guarantee without extrapolating from finite tests.
