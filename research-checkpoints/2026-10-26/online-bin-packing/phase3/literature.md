# Phase3 primary-source reuse and scope

The native continuation begins from the retrieved external note and independent audit in `extra-high/packing-round1/`; those are internal research artifacts, not established literature.

Primary foundations:

* [A New Lower Bound for Classic Online Bin Packing](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.05554), Theorem1 and Sections2–4. The adaptive separator accepts observable occupancy predicates. It gives multiplicative separation within a round; it does not automatically compare absolute widths across earlier rounds. The published t=3 batch sizes and offline constructions were reused directly.
* [A New and Improved Algorithm for Online Bin Packing](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.01728), the unchanged AH class/parameter/weight machinery. The new type-resolved analysis starts from the independently audited phase2 tiny Best Fit chronology lemma; it does not silently substitute a different online model.

The current literature anchors and original exact AH certificate/version checks remain in the preserved earlier literature notes. This phase establishes no priority claim. The new numerical upper bound is small, independently checked internally, and still requires external human/formal review. Restricted adversary survival, restricted analysis optimality, and actual algorithm competitive bounds are distinguished throughout.
