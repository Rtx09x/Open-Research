This is the lean core of the 16 October 2026 reviewed package. “Reviewed” means the documented research/editorial checking process, not independent human peer review or publication acceptance. AI assistance was used in that workflow. No new license is granted.

## Scope

The paper studies finite-state transitions under a repeated quasistatic scalar pulse, with at most one rising and one falling individual switch. It gives realizability, memory, interaction-degree and support results in that specific threshold model. It does not establish manufacturing cost, energy use, thermal robustness, physical implementability of arbitrary coefficients, or control of all off-protocol initial conditions.

The mathematical proofs are in the manuscript. Exact computations support finite constructions, lower-bound certificates and classifications; they are not a formal verification of every all-size theorem or a novelty/priority certification.

## Build and check

Work in a disposable copy: scientific programs regenerate result files beside their sources.

```sh
bash scripts/verify.sh
bash scripts/build_pdf.sh
```

The check suite needs Python 3.10 or later, assertions enabled, and only the standard library. It runs 15 retained programs. Do not use `python -O`.

The PDF build needs a configured TeX Live installation with `pdflatex`, `bibtex`, TikZ/PGF, AMS packages, `mathtools`, `natbib`, `cleveref`, `microtype`, `enumitem`, `booktabs`, `caption`, `hyperref` and `float`. The script runs LaTeX, BibTeX, then LaTeX twice. All figures are native sources; no external image conversion or network access is required. Generated bibliography/build files are deliberately omitted.

## What is retained

The three `repro` groups keep their original relative layout so imports and fixture paths remain valid. Their names identify research stages, not alternative manuscript versions:

- `original`: exact event simulator, decimal-counter lower-bound certificates, capacity witnesses and regression/reset checks.
- `generalizations`: unary maps, resets/merges, cycle composition, the 1,000-state construction and exact regression fixtures.
- `audit`: independent model validation, resource/support checks, seven-state separation, rational certificate checking and complete small-map classification inputs.

Only required input witnesses/certificates and their checkers are committed. Outputs are regenerated by the runner. Historical ZIP bundles, older papers, duplicate exports, solver/discovery programs, unrelated explorations, repeated reports and raw logs remain in the separate full backup. The manuscript's references to the broader original review archive do not mean those historical materials are part of this lean core.
