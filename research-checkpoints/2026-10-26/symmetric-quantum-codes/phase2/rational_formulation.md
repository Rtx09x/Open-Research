# Rational coefficients, compact domain, and endpoint reduction

`exact_moment_coefficients.json` contains the complete exact Q(i) coefficient data. It uses spin9 on the monomial basis, J_+|w>=(18-w)|w+1>, J_-|w>=w|w-1>, with Hilbert metric diag(1/binomial(18,w)). Thirty-six unnormalized Hermitian harmonic tensors are generated by lowering commutators of J_+^r, r<=5; B0=I. The exporter verifies weighted Hermiticity, orthogonality, and all324 products B_a B_b and B_a J_i B_b exactly. The advisor independently replayed all data with standard-library rational complex arithmetic, without worker imports or SymPy.

Moments ell(B_k) are scalar x_k I2 through rank4, and arbitrary Hermitian2-by-2 for rank5. Define the scalar9-by-9 G and the18-by-18 K_i directly from the exported product coefficients. Put

    M=I_spinor ⊗ (G ⊗ I_logical),
    Ttilde=[[9G⊗I2-K_z,K_x-iK_y],[K_x+iK_y,9G⊗I2+K_z]],
    Tbar=M^-1 Ttilde, Sbar=19Tbar-Tbar².

These entries are rational functions of the68 real moments, with fixed Q(i) coefficients. Tbar is similar to the Hermitian T; it is not itself ordinarily Hermitian. Its trace invariants, including Pauli-inserted traces in F, agree with those of T because the similarity commutes with the spinor Pauli matrices.

The positivity condition is **not** Sbar>=0 in the ordinary inner product. It is

    19Ttilde-Ttilde M^-1 Ttilde >=0,

or equivalently the two **linear** matrix inequalities

    Ttilde>=0, 19M-Ttilde>=0,

with G>0. Denominators can be cleared by positive powers of detG for a polynomial certificate.

## Universal endpoint kernels
The18 operators a B_a,b B_a span rectangular tensor ranks1/2,3/2,5/2, of total dimension12. Hence Ttilde has12 universal null vectors after logical doubling. Creation operators give12 universal null vectors for19M-Ttilde. These two kernel spaces are M-orthogonal and disjoint whenG>0. The advisor checked their explicit rational syzygies and all36 cross-kernel operator identities independently.

Consequently T always has twelve zero eigenvalues and twelve eigenvalues19. Its nontrivial12-by-12 block has trace96. For an actual18-qubit code, that block must have spectrum

    0×6, 19×4, 10+|gamma|, 10-|gamma|.

The common metric-orthogonal complement depends only on the24 low scalar moments. This gives a smaller interface for structured semidefinite/SOS work, not an exclusion.

## Exact compact bounds
In the original HS-orthonormal error chart, TrG=9/19. From the audited variance upper bound and trace-zero deviation,

    ||G-I/19||_F²<=1/722,
    1/57 <= lambda_min(G) <= lambda_max(G) <=5/57.

The improved endpoint estimate uses |delta_i|² <=(8/9) Σdelta_j² for nine real deviations summing to zero.

`domain_bounds.py/json` supplies two additional exact intrinsic-LP dual certificates:

    a5<=1/20,  a5+(Ha)5=2(Ma)5<=24/5.

Thus normalized rank-five scalar moments have squared norm<=1/380; all four logical Pauli components together have squared norm<=24/95. For the unnormalized basis with h_k=Tr(B_k²), replace each squared normalized moment by x_k²/h_k. The low-moment ellipsoid is

    nu=Σ_(r=1)^4 c_r Σ_(rank B_k=r) x_k²/h_k,
    1/102400 < nu <=1/722.

These compact-domain bounds are useful for certification; they do not by themselves contradict feasibility.
