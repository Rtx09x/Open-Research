# Priority and scope audit: the fully symmetric 18-qubit exclusion

**Evidence cutoff:** 25 October 2026 (UTC). **Audit type:** adversarial literature/prior-art check, not a rerun of the mathematical certificate. **Disposition:** no independently verifiable prior unrestricted exclusion at n = 18 was located in the sources inspected. The concrete finite result is a plausible new contribution, but this search does **not** establish exhaustive novelty or absence of unpublished work.

## Findings first

1. **The genuinely incremental statement is the n = 18 exclusion**, for every complex two-dimensional subspace of Sym^18(C²), with no parity, reality, support, gate-covariance, or anticoherence ansatz. The established shorter exclusions and established 19-qubit construction supply the remaining endpoints; neither should be presented as new.
2. **There are earlier “minimal 19” assertions that must be acknowledged.** Aydin–Alekseyev–Barg's published Section 7 contains potentially broad wording in the context of a restricted real, odd-length ansatz. Visnevskyi's July 2025 MSc thesis reports the same numerical thresholds later used by Bond et al., and includes an overstrong inference from unsuccessful homotopy searches. Neither inspected source supplied a checkable unrestricted complex n = 18 nonexistence certificate. This is a qualification about evidence, not a claim that the authors did not possess additional calculations.
3. **Generic multi-copy/PPT/SDP methodology is definitely prior art**, including Yu et al. (2021, 2022), not just Anglès Munné–Nemec–Huber (2024/2025). The contribution must be framed as a particular finite symmetry-reduced relaxation and rigorous separating witness, not invention of a complete hierarchy or the multi-copy idea.
4. **The latest relevant revisions and October 2026 follow-ons did not close the gap in the inspected material.** In particular, the October deletion/insertion equivalence update, October code-switching preprint, and 20 October bosonic-code publication address different questions or regimes.
5. **This settles only t = 2 if the supplied mathematical audit is accepted.** It does not establish the all-t formula 3t²+3t+1.

## Scope being tested

The target is an orthogonal rank-two projector P supported on the spin-9 irreducible representation, equivalently Sym^18(C²), satisfying scalar compression of all physical Pauli operators of weight at most four. This is distance at least five and correction of arbitrary errors on up to two qubits. Equivalence with intrinsic depth five is already formalized by Kubischta–Teixeira, Appendix B. A code projector commuting with permutations is not sufficient: every code vector must be fully symmetric.

The internal inputs read were `symmetric-quantum-codes/phase2/theorem_and_necessity.md` and `quantum-independent-audit/INDEPENDENT_APPLICABILITY_REPORT.md`. They identify certificate SHA-256 `201e1acd2e8616807d98d07038174559cc4480aba1cca8b2d736997e251c7a79` and report an unrestricted-complex applicability PASS. This audit accepts that premise. No frozen proof artifacts were changed.

## 1. Direct competitors and the exact finite gap

### Okada: the important prior lower bound, but not n = 18

**Rui Samuel Okada**, *A Quantum Analog of Delsarte's Linear Programming Bounds*, UC Davis PhD thesis, December 2023; corrected arXiv release **2502.14165v1, 20 February 2025**, currently the only arXiv version. Chapter 3, Table 3.1 (printed p. 89) gives dimension upper bounds of 1 at n = 13,…,17 and distance five; earlier columns exclude shorter cases. Crucially its n = 18, distance-five entry is **2.019**, followed by **2.096** at n = 19. Thus this published computation does not exclude dimension two at 18. Section 3.4 discusses exact rational GLPK checks for smaller cases; self-dual/shadow inequalities are already incorporated. The original deposited 2023 table has the same entries, but the corrected 2025 version should be used for the proof dependency because it corrects Lemma 3.2.5. The table was visually checked, not inferred solely from text extraction. [Corrected version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14165v1), [original thesis](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/6jg5h7cj).

### Aydin–Alekseyev–Barg: known existence and an ambiguous priority claim

*A family of permutationally invariant quantum codes*, **2310.05358v1 (9 October 2023), v2 (6 February 2024), v3 (15 April 2024)**; Quantum 8, 1321 (30 April 2024). All three versions were inspected. Section 7 reports an msolve interval solution at n = 19, checked with Mathematica. Its Proposition 7.1 assumes odd n, real coefficients, and a parity-separated reversal ansatz. The surrounding remark that no shorter two-error code exists is insufficiently qualified if read in isolation; it cannot simply be erased from the priority discussion. The inspected text supplies no unrestricted even-length/complex exclusion argument. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.22331/q-2024-04-30-1321), [exact latest preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05358v3).

### Visnevskyi's 2025 thesis: an earlier numerical antecedent requiring attribution

**Vladyslav Visnevskyi**, *High-dimensional permutation-invariant quantum error-correcting codes*, MSc Physics and Astronomy, University of Amsterdam, **29 July 2025**. The repository records its addition on **22 August 2025** (commit `08bec93e500a317a7c637ae3883cce8b7001624c`), establishing public availability before Bond et al. Section 3.2, Tables 1–2, reports thresholds 7, 19, 37, 61, 91 and Conjecture 3.2 gives the quadratic law. A sentence treats failure of HomotopyContinuation to find solutions as nonexistence; Section 3.1 itself distinguishes finding an instance from characterizing underdetermined solution sets. In the inspected companion homotopy file, the codewords are related by the PR reversal map, coefficients are floating point, several coefficients are fixed to zero, and selected real solutions are extracted. No validated completeness log or unrestricted n = 18 certificate was found there. **Classification: prior computational assertion and conjectural numerical evidence, not a verified unrestricted theorem.** [Thesis at pinned repository commit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Techtonick/PI-codes/e30da4371caa5608bf727705121abb4cc26d67c6/High_dimensional_PI_Error_correcting_codes.pdf).

### Bond et al.: numerical conjecture, not an exclusion certificate

**Bond, Minář, Ozols, Safavi-Naini, Visnevskyi**, *Permutation-invariant codes: a numerical study and qudit constructions*, **2603.10981v1, 11 March 2026**, only version. Section 3.1 searches complex coefficients for t = 1,2,3 and PR coefficients through t = 5; accepted solutions have small objective and gradient, with finite random restarts. Conjecture 3.1 explicitly states the proposed minimum. Some “minimal” prose should therefore be read as numerical observation, not a proof. Unsuccessful local optimization cannot establish global nonexistence. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10981v1).

## 3. Alternative terminology and related bounds

- **Anticoherent subspaces / joint higher-rank numerical ranges / quantum anticliques:** Pereira–Paul-Paddock, *Anticoherent Subspaces*, **1610.05841v1 (19 October 2016), v2 (1 June 2017)**, Theorem 4 gives the joint-rank numerical-range characterization. It constrains traceless low-rank multipoles to compress to **zero**. General KL codes allow nonzero scalar compressions. Therefore an anticoherence-only no-go would not alone prove the target. No spin-9/order-four/two-dimensional exclusion was located. [v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05841v2).
- **Published anticoherence dimension bound:** Serrano-Ensástiga–Chryssomalakos–Martin, *Quantum metrology of rotations with mixed spin states*, **2404.15548v1 (23 April 2024), v2 (11 May 2024), v3 (27 February 2025)**, PRA 111, 022435. Eq. (52) bounds k by floor((2j−t+1)/(t+1)); at j = 9, t = 4 it allows k = 3, so it cannot exclude k = 2 even within the stronger AC class. Its numerical study covers orders one and two and j ≤ 17/2. [Latest version](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15548v3).
- **Theses under the alternate terminology:** Jérôme Denis, *Bridging Concepts of Quantumness: Phase-space, Entanglement and Anticoherence in Spin Systems*, Liège PhD, title-page academic year **2025–2026**, §§4.1.3 and 4.2.2, proves AC-subspace sufficiency for low-degree spin-error correction, not a dimension-two spin-9 exclusion. [Institutional PDF](https://orbi.uliege.be/bitstream/2268/336540/1/JD_Thesis_Final.pdf). Ana Gabriela Flores Delgado, *Kinematics in quantum state space: geometric phases and anticoherence*, UNAM PhD, **February 2025**, develops k-plane geometry and anticoherence optimization; no equivalent target result was located in relevant sections. [Institutional PDF](https://tesiunamdocumentos.dgb.unam.mx/ptd2025/ene_mar/0866016/0866016.pdf).
- **Earlier restricted exclusions:** Pollatsek–Ruskai's **quant-ph/0304153v1 (24 April 2003), v2 (27 January 2004), v3 (20 July 2004)**, published LAA 392 (2004), excludes certain short PR codes and constructs seven-qubit codes. Its assumptions and specialized two-error types must not be inflated into an unrestricted distance-five lower bound at 18. [Published primary source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2004.06.014).
- **General Singleton comparison:** n ≥ 4t+1 gives only n ≥ 9 at t = 2 (also displayed as Eq. (12) in Bond et al.). The strong relevant finite lower bound is Okada's n ≥ 18. A nondegenerate Hamming argument cannot silently be applied to the unrestricted, potentially degenerate PI problem.

**Secondary inconsistency worth avoiding:** Pereira–Paul-Paddock v2 Corollary 2 states a largest one-anticoherent-subspace dimension floor(s+1), whereas the later Eq. (52) at t = 1 gives floor(j). These formulas conflict for the same spin label. This audit does not adjudicate that separate issue and relies on neither for the n = 18 conclusion.

## 7. Recommended research positioning

Use: **“We give a computer-assisted exclusion of every complex two-dimensional distance-five code in Sym^18(C²). Together with the prior shorter-length bounds and the known 19-qubit construction, this establishes n_min^PI(2) = 19.”**

Then distinguish: rigorous prior lower bounds and constructions; numerical evidence and earlier broad assertions; established generic hierarchy techniques; and the new finite certificate. Avoid “first SDP approach,” “first lower bound,” “all-t conjecture solved,” or an unqualified “no one proved this before.” The accompanying `CITATION_ADDENDUM.md` provides a concise attribution-only insertion for the theorem writeup. A fresh literature check near public release would still be prudent; it is not necessary to reopen or modify the frozen certificate to make these attribution corrections.
