# Clarification for the earlier auxiliary complement-count note

All syndrome dimensions and Schwinger compressions in `complement_rank_counting.md` refer to **intrinsic collective tensor operators acting within the symmetric spin irrep V_n**. They do not refer to the full physical Pauli-error image space, since individual physical errors can leave V_n. The intrinsic degree≤t operator space has dimension(t+1)²; full physical error images can have a different, larger dimension.

The full-rank intrinsic Gram hypothesis remains conditional. The complement-count note is only a barrier to naive counting and is not used in either finite exclusion certificate. The actual n33/n34 proof detects every physical error product of combined support≤6 through the established symmetric-space operator-system equivalence; no physical error sector is discarded by assumption.
